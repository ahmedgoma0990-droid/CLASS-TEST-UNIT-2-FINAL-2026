Thomas Edison: The Great Inventor

Thomas Edison was one of the most famous inventors in history. He was born in Ohio, USA, in 1847. When he was a young boy, he loved to ask questions about everything. His teacher thought he asked too many questions and got angry with him, so his mother took him out of school and taught him at home.

Edison had a problem with his hearing, but he did not let that stop him. He worked very hard all his life. He is famous for inventing the light bulb. But he also invented the phonograph (a machine that plays music) and an early type of movie camera. People called him "The Wizard of Menlo Park" because of his amazing inventions.

Edison always said, "Genius is one percent inspiration and ninety-nine percent perspiration." This means that success is mostly about hard work, not just being smart. Thomas Edison died in 1931, but his inventions changed the world forever.
